# Ecology Letters Perspective venue check

Checked: 2026-08-31 against the current Wiley *Ecology Letters* Author Guidelines and the journal editorial note on Perspective/Synthesis proposals.

Current guidance relevant to the boundary-paper route:

- unsolicited Perspective proposals are evaluated before a full submission; a full Perspective submitted without invitation or prior proposal approval will not be considered;
- the proposal must be **one paragraph**, no more than **300 words**, and describe the nature and novelty of the work and its contribution to the discipline;
- the journal editorial note additionally recommends stating the qualifications of the proposed author(s);
- proposals are sent by email to **both** current Editorial Office addresses: `ecolets@cefe.cnrs.fr` and `ecolets2@cefe.cnrs.fr`;
- Perspective papers have the same novelty expectation as Letters, should appeal to a wide ecological audience, and should not be principally a review, a statement of opinion, or a discussion of the author's own work;
- successful Perspectives usually present a quantitative analysis as a way of introducing a new perspective in ecology;
- full Perspective manuscripts allow up to **7,500 main-text words** and up to **10 figures/tables/text boxes**;
- the Perspective abstract limit is **200 words**;
- for a later full submission, the title page must include article type, author information, a running title under 45 characters, up to 10 keywords, word/reference/figure counts, authorship statement, data accessibility statement and correspondence details.

Repository implication: the proposal should lead with the quantitative `k-1-r` equivalence-dimension rule and the `Gamma` calibration-transport family, with pollination as a broad ecological application rather than presenting the manuscript as a review of the author's own programme. The author-qualification sentence should establish ability to deliver the proposed quantitative framework without making the proposal primarily autobiographical.

Re-check immediately before sending because journal instructions and editorial addresses can change.
