# Ecology Letters Perspective venue check

Checked: 2026-08-31.

Current journal guidance relevant to the boundary-paper route:

- unsolicited Perspective proposals are evaluated before a full submission;
- the proposal must be no more than 300 words and describe the nature/novelty of the work and its contribution to the discipline;
- Perspective papers have the same novelty expectation as Letters and should not be primarily review or opinion pieces;
- successful Perspectives usually present quantitative analysis introducing a new ecological perspective;
- full Perspective manuscripts allow up to 7,500 main-text words and up to 10 figures/tables/text boxes; the Perspective abstract limit is 200 words;
- the proposal should be sent to the Ecology Letters Editorial Office at the two addresses listed in the current author guidelines.

Repository implication: the proposal should lead with the quantitative `k-1-r` equivalence-dimension rule and the `Gamma` calibration-transport family, with pollination as a broad ecological application rather than presenting the manuscript as a review of the author's own programme.

Source checked against the current Ecology Letters Wiley author guidelines and the journal editorial note on Perspective/Synthesis proposals. Re-check immediately before sending because journal instructions can change.
