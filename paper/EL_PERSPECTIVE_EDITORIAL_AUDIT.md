# Ecology Letters Perspective — editorial desk-rejection audit

Status: send-gate audit for the boundary-paper proposal.

## Editorial question 1 — Why is this a Perspective rather than a Method?

**Risk.** The proposal contains equations, code and measurement rules, so it can be read as a statistical/methods contribution.

**Required answer.** The paper changes the ecological interpretation of common composite measurements before any particular estimator is chosen. Its central claim is that a measured product defines an observational equivalence class, and that the dimension of that class determines what additional field measurements are required. The code instantiates the perspective; it is not the primary contribution.

**Send criterion.** The proposal must say that the contribution is a measurement-design perspective about what ecological observations can identify, not a new estimator or software package.

## Editorial question 2 — What is genuinely new if the algebra is elementary?

**Risk.** `W=prod_j F_j` and proxy-calibration algebra are elementary and connect to established identifiability/partial-identification traditions.

**Required answer.** Novelty is the closed ecological consequence: product-preserving equivalence dimension (`k-1-r`), calibration-transport sensitivity and breakdown, and the resulting field-design/reporting rules. Do not claim invention of identifiability algebra.

**Send criterion.** The proposal must state the novelty positively without selling mathematical depth.

## Editorial question 3 — Is the scope broad enough for general ecology?

**Risk.** Pollination could make the paper look like a subfield critique of visitation/network proxies.

**Required answer.** Pollination is the lead concrete example; seed dispersal provides an independent quantity-by-quality architecture, and the general result applies to declared positive multiplicative chains in recruitment, survival and other multi-stage ecological processes.

**Send criterion.** The proposal must state that pollination is an application, not the scope boundary.

## Editorial question 4 — Does it merely review the author's programme?

**Risk.** Ecology Letters explicitly excludes Perspectives that primarily discuss the author's own work.

**Required answer.** The proposal should pitch a general ecological measurement problem supported by established pollination and seed-dispersal literatures. Author qualification belongs in one short closing clause only. RACH/NOV/G2 and the separate MEE paper must not enter the pitch.

**Send criterion.** Author qualification <= one sentence; no RACH/NOV/G2 claims.

## Editorial question 5 — What changes for a field ecologist tomorrow?

**Risk.** Equivalence classes can sound abstract.

**Required answer.** The framework answers two operational questions: how many independent channel measurements are needed to eliminate a given number of unresolved dimensions, and how much calibration-transport failure is sufficient to overturn a directional conclusion. It also prohibits independently combinable channel error bars when one calibration ratio couples them.

**Send criterion.** At least one anchor-count consequence and one calibration/reporting consequence must appear in the proposal.

## Editorial question 6 — Why now?

**Risk.** The problem is timeless, so the proposal may lack topical urgency.

**Required answer.** Contemporary ecology routinely combines network summaries, proxies and multi-stage observation chains across changing environments. The framework targets exactly the contrasts in which proxy transport and missing links become consequential. Avoid unsupported trend claims; make the timeliness about present measurement practice and cross-system transfer.

## Final send gate

The proposal is send-ready only if all are true:

- one paragraph;
- <=300 words;
- nature, novelty, disciplinary contribution and author qualification explicit;
- Perspective-vs-Method distinction explicit;
- `k-1-r` anchor consequence present;
- `Gamma`/breakdown or equivalent calibration consequence present;
- pollination framed as an application, with an independent cross-domain architecture named;
- no claim of new identifiability algebra;
- no RACH/NOV/G2 claim;
- email copy exactly matches the canonical proposal text;
- both current Ecology Letters Editorial Office recipients present.
