# Repository scope — Methods paper only

This repository owns one active publication programme:

> **Mechanism-Resolving Observation Design** — an information-theoretic method for retaining an admissible ecological mechanism region and selecting observations that reduce residual mechanism ambiguity.

The separate Perspective on mechanistic evidence and identification boundaries is owned by:

`https://github.com/zuizui0223/boundary`

## Ownership rule

Active content in this repository may support:

- admissible mechanism-region inference;
- mechanism entropy, resolvability, equivalence and replaceability;
- observation information value `V(Q)=I(S;Q|A_epsilon)/K`;
- sequential observation design;
- controlled truth-peek-free validation;
- methods-paper figures, reviewer bundle and software release.

Active content in this repository must not contain the standalone Paper A manuscript, its Ecology Letters proposal, calibration-transport theory, multichannel identification theorem, Paper A figures or Paper A reviewer bundle.

## Historical material

Pre-split archives may retain old names and integrated drafts for provenance. Frozen G2 protocol/result files may retain historical machine identifiers and policy keys. Those historical strings do not define the active method name.

## No double counting

Results owned by the external `boundary` repository are not presented here as primary contributions. Conversely, the frozen observation-selection benchmark and the methods-paper software validation belong to Mechanism-Resolving Observation Design and are not primary evidence for the Perspective.
